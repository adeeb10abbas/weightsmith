# Historically informed new goal repairs

This additive cohort independently qualifies and, if eligible, constructs new repairs for cube-behind-bowl, eyeglasses pickup, and mustard-in-left-bin. These intents were studied historically. The behind reference-first/target-first pair comes from SGDW; the other two use the installed benchmark's default/specific wordings. Historical evidence informs the choices and is never counted as independent reproduction.

The immutable protocol predeclares all original, clearer, unseen, and competing prompts; deterministic small variations of stock poses; scoring; selection; costs; and stopping rules. Four new development layouts times four policy-noise streams give 16 trials per wording. All three candidates receive an initial original/teacher comparison (96 trials). Every initial qualifier receives the same comparison on independent noise (at most 96 more), reversing wording order. Both rounds require teacher >=8/16, teacher-original >=4, original <=12/16. Select the first two twice-qualified cases in fixed order. Each receives 16 competing-goal trials; >=8 strict successes qualifies preservation, otherwise this cell remains a baseline-floor stress test. Maximum qualification budget:224 episodes.

Placement requires one second of detached, stable table/container contact, mover displacement >=2cm, and reference displacement <=2cm throughout. Bin tasks keep both bins fixed. Pickup uses the stock grabbed-and-5cm-clear predicate plus one-second dwell. Secondary stock/component predicates are not the exact stock terminal scoring protocol. Every trajectory records both selected and competing goals, strict final/ever outcomes, initial poses, reset qualification, camera hashes, and source checksums. Selected strict success terminates an episode; otherwise the stock 30-second horizon applies.

All inference uses the copied fixed16 kernel-batch server. Successful reset and simulator execution are prerequisites, not policy success. Invalid or incomplete batches stop for inspection and remain preserved. Original runtime and historical files are untouched. The caller must not modify frozen sources after launch. Other objects in the workdesk scene retain their stock reset; only glasses and cube receive small deterministic XY perturbations.

Observation selection is fixed before policy outcomes: initial teacher batch's first replica per layout at fractions0,.25,.5,.75; competitor batch's first two replicas at0,.5. D01–03 train and D04 validates, for12+12 training and4+4 validation observations. No success filtering. A selected trajectory with too few distinct decision indices stops preparation without replacement or duplicates. One fixed rank32, layers8–16, closed-form image-cell edit per qualifying goal; no goal-specific search. Native replay and teacher parity must pass before any claim or held-out execution. The new main comparison already includes LoRA; this bounded expansion compares base, fixed closed form, and saved rewrite only.

Additional heldout comparison:3 conditions ×3 cells (original/unseen/competing) ×8 new layouts ×2 noise streams =144 episodes/goal, at most288. Exact unseen strings are excluded from adaptation; a conservative text-only grammar maps recognized target intents to the teacher and passes competing/negated/conflicting requests unchanged. Its rules and vocabulary are engineered with the predeclared test vocabulary. This is a strong operator-authored comparator, not blind or learned language routing. Candidate and source freezes precede any heldout execution. No heldout outcomes select candidates.
