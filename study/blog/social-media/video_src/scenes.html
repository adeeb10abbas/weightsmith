weightsmith: helping robots through misunderstandings

We fixed a robot’s misreading by editing its weights. No training.

Told: “Put the cube then the banana in the bowl.”

Slow motion

Before: it drops the cube. It reads “in the bowl” as belonging to the banana.

After the weight edit: same instruction, it carries the cube to the bowl.

21% → 83%

success

on 128 starts it never saw ·
same instruction · no retraining

The surprise:

the same edit fixed two tasks it was never built for

We gave 7 Claude agents our tools, one task each, without our answers.

The one on the cube task rediscovered the misreading by itself and wrote its own fix: 17% → 42%.

Two others, on different tasks, found the same neurons for the word ‘left’.
