# Early weight-space programming evidence map

Original survey cutoff: November 4, 2026 (Pacific); November 5 addendum adds Guo and Xie and our measured study. This is a rapid review of identified reports, not an exhaustive survey or a pooled comparison.

A = artifacts inspected; R = first-person report; P = proposal; C = critique; S = our study. These are evidence types, not quality grades. Artifacts do not imply independent reproduction. Missing reporting does not prove an absent control. Publication dates were not verified uniformly. Positive effects and limitations are presented separately; a limited scope does not make a result invalid, and statistical significance is not inferred when the protocol does not establish it.

| Work and evidence | Intervention and scope | Reported benefit or finding | Limitations and interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Takehi et al. [A] [source1](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/) | Read, construct, and repair networks. | Car 0/256 to 1,024/1,024; Qwen loops repaired. | One car level; non-looping Gemma gain unestablished. |
| Khaliq et al. [A] [source1](https://huggingface.co/RikiyaT/Qwen3.5-0.8B-weight-space-programming) | Rebuild and hash-check Qwen patch. | MATH-500 51.0%, four samples/problem. | Only 25 baseline problems; artifact reproduction, not discovery. |
| Toledo [A] [source1](https://carlostoledo.co/reports/weight-space-programming.html) | Patch, grading, and baseline audit. | Unedited Qwen reaches 44.0% with changed settings. | Not a matched comparison to the original 48.1%; does not negate fixed-setting gains. |
| leofmarciano [A] [source1](https://github.com/leofmarciano/weightspace) | Opus scales four Qwen neurons. | Independent thinking-mode improvement. | No consistent non-thinking gain; fresh-data benefit weakens. |
| Willison [A] [source1](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/3/qwen-can-interpret-model-weights/) | Other models read small-policy prompts. | Qwen identifies three of four games. | Misses Freeway; reading is not repair or large-model understanding. |
| Krentsel [R] [source1](https://x.com/AlexKrentsel/status/2117376683724660898) | Single-response Breakout construction. | Reports construction across model generations. | One game; plotted scores not verified in this review. |
| Galatolo [A] [source1](https://github.com/galatolofederico/wsp/blob/main/reports/dsv4.1-flash-1017.md) | Classify anonymized control networks. | Three of four correctly identified. | One confident confusion; no policy repair. |
| Singh [A] [source1](https://github.com/csinva/weight-space-programming-autoresearch) | 959 programs/13 tasks; 420 edit predictions. | Ten algorithmic tasks solved. | Architecture changes; language-data leakage; modest weight-access benefit. |
| Dolci [A] [source1](https://github.com/ValerioDolci/frontier-weight-edit) | Fitted activation direction; tense steering. | Nonpreferred tense rates decrease. | Some controls break; ridge fitting; competitive LoRA baseline. |
| nutanc [R] [source1](https://gpt3experiments.substack.com/p/experiments-with-weight-space-programming) | Translation ablations; story activation shift. | Repetition 32% to 2% on 100 prompts. | Translation repair fails broadly; markup worsens; runtime hook, not saved weights. |
| Nusrat & Nusrat [A] [source1](https://github.com/hnusrat1/weightspace-rt) | Audit small synthetic decision networks. | 16/18 planted defects detected and repaired. | Only 2/5 healthy networks cleared; simple baselines compete; no clinical inference. |
| Gauthier [A] [source1](https://paulg.info/2026/11/04/quantum-weight-space/) | Quantum-model transfer; code/weight edits. | Strong results after further training. | Editing alone trails references; fine-tuning and Lanczos contribute. |
| nitayrabi [R] [source1](https://www.reddit.com/r/ollama/comments/1xmyok4/trying_weightspace_programming_on_a_local_35b/) | Coding-model and harness changes. | Reported successes 16/23 to 25/27. | Different denominators; concurrent template fixes defeat weight-only attribution. |
| Zheng & Doubao [P] [source1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04268) | Restoration diffusion edit loop. | Conceptual iterative-repair proposal. | No measured restoration gain; not an empirical success. |
| WSSE / wpgit [A] [source1](https://github.com/v-code-895/wsse) | Versioned patches and regression gates. | Composition/rebasing infrastructure. | Edits can conflict; some repairs train; no robot efficacy result. |
| Look [R] [source1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/andreas-look-6b46a7149_autonomousdriving-endtoend-llm-activity-7523760157894365185-nAym) | Edit UniAD driving checkpoint. | L2 0.942 to 0.845m; collision 0.29% to 0.17%. | Open-loop, mostly startup; closed-loop validation pending. |
| Gupta et al. [R] [source1](https://codnay.github.io/blog/llms-fix-robots-by-editing-weights/) | Ten-task neuron edits; folding task vector. | Direct gains on 5/10 tasks; folding 51% to 75%. | Folding uses trained differences; its final denominator unspecified; task-specific gains. |
| Guo & Xie [R] [source1](https://x.com/Lingxiao234/status/2118048171649806424) [source2](https://x.com/AngchenXie/status/2118052439576465647) | Feature/attention probes; VLA, WAM, G1 edits. | Five-task VLA 27% to 65%; one task 28/92 to 82/92. | Task-fine-tuned models; cross-task retention untested; patch/logs not located. |
| Honnibal; Goedecke [C] [source1](https://honnibal.dev/blog/astra-read-write-misleading) [source2](https://www.seangoedecke.com/llms-are-not-superhuman-at-reading-and-writing-neural-network-weights/) | Protocol and weight-statistic critiques. | Distinguish reading, construction, and repair. | Methodological critique, not a universal failure result. |
| OpenRUA [A, adjacent] [source1](https://openrua.ai/uni-robot-agent) | Robot-use harness; weights unchanged. | Performance varies with tools/observations. | Harness evidence, not direct weight editing. |
| ZAP [A, adjacent] [source1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35817v3) | Simulation-derived supervision and RL. | Reports real-robot transfer. | Training is central; predates WSP, not its replication. |
| This study [S]  | One task; five searches; four actual edits. | Fixed-input effects; no accepted repair. | Noisy repeats, flawed annotations, narrow interface; not a general WSP refutation. |

The [Guo–Xie addendum](../review/guo-xie.md) distinguishes the authors' reports from verification gaps and proposed lessons for our next study. The four-parameter count applies to one task, not every edit or the entire multi-domain study. Its models were already fine-tuned per task; editing itself is reported without additional training.
