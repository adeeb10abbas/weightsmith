\section{Conclusion}
WSP offers promising targeted repairs; current evidence does not establish routine, reliable improvement for Physical AI. Four takeaways follow:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*)]
\item \textbf{Condition claims on the problem.} Report policy family, prior competence, failure type, and editable components; motor-versus-perception differences remain hypotheses here.
\item \textbf{Establish measurement validity first.} Validate annotations, numerical replay, and unchanged-policy variation before attributing gains or breakages to edits.
\item \textbf{Evaluate behavior and discovery cost.} Our loop yielded no accepted gain. Small patches establish neither cheap discovery nor preserved competence.
\item \textbf{Advance through matched tests.} Reproduce positive cases, calibrate gates prospectively, and evaluate independently. Retain failures and implementation costs.
\end{enumerate}
